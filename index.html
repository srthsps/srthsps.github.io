WISEWORDS

A crossword that grows into the sky.

Swipe letters to solve each ledge, watch the vine climb higher, and learn a new word with every swipe. For kids and grown-ups, in seven languages.

How it plays

Swipe, climb, learn

1

Swipe the letters

Join letters on the wheel to spell the words hidden in the ledge. Find extra words for bonus coins.

2

Grow the vine

Solved words turn to stone and a new ledge sprouts above. No level screens, no timers, no losing.

3

Learn the words

Tap any word for its meaning. Every word you find goes into your own Word Book.

A new surprise in every world

Eight kinds of special tiles

Ice freezes a word until its neighbour is solved. Bombs uncover letters. Mystery tiles change with every try. Fog, golden buds, lightning and moss join as you climb through five worlds, from Sunny Meadow to Starry Night.

  • Ice
  • Bombs
  • Mystery tiles
  • Fog
  • Golden buds
  • Lightning
  • Moss
  • Critters

Every day

A new puzzle each morning

The Daily Bloom is one extra puzzle a day, the same for everyone. Fill your calendar, keep your streak and win a new flower frame every month. Lexi has a little gift for you every day too.

Learn as you play

Every word has a story

See what any solved word means, and hunt down 92 rare treasure words, each with a riddle, similar words and where the word comes from.

From ledge 71

Catch all five critters

They hop around the vine with every try. Solve the word one sits on to catch it for your collection.
